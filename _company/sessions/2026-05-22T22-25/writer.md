### 3. 썸네일 이미지 생성용 최종 프롬프트 (Designer 에게 전달)
- **목표**: 클릭률(CTR)을 극대화하는 강렬하고 아름다운 이미지 생성.
- **스타일**: 고희스트, 미학적, 브랜드 컬러 강조.

**[프롬프트 텍스트]**
> "A cinematic shot of a deep indigo night sky with soft gold rays of moonlight breaking through clouds, illuminating a serene modern city skyline in the distance. The image should have a dreamy, ethereal quality, reminiscent of a high-end fashion editorial or a luxury travel magazine cover. Use a shallow depth of field to focus on the foreground where a lone figure stands silhouetted against the glowing horizon, looking towards the lights. Color palette: Deep Indigo (#1a0d3e) as primary background, Soft Gold (#ffd700) for lighting accents and highlights. High contrast but soft edges. Photorealistic texture with subtle grain. No text on image."
